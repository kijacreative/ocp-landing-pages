The fine print, made short

What happens if I don't choose?

On January 1, your membership automatically moves to All-Studio Unlimited at $229 a month.

How long is my rate locked?

Choose before December 31, 2026 and your rate holds through December 31, 2027. If rates rise again after that, this rate isn't guaranteed.

Can I pause?

Yes, on every plan. Minimum 7 days, maximum 3 months.

Do my teacher or nurse discounts still apply?

Yes, on monthly plans. Teacher, nurse and other discounts take $10 off per month. They don't apply to prepay.

How does the guest pass work?

Every plan includes a free guest pass each month to bring a friend to class.
