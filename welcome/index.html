02 · Before your first class

Five things worth knowing

  1. 01

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    Tell the front desk it's your first time. A trainer will set your springs and footbar and walk you through the reformer before class starts.

  2. 02

    Bring grip socks

    They're required on the reformer. Bring your own, or grab a pair at the front desk.

  3. 03

    Wear something fitted

    Leggings and a fitted top. Loose shorts and baggy tees catch on the springs and straps.

  4. 04

    Eat light, drink water

    Skip a big meal in the hour before. Bring a water bottle and you're set.

  5. 05

    Go at your own pace

    Every class is 45 minutes and every move has an easier option. Nobody is watching you. They're all busy with their own reformer.

06 · First-timer questions

Asked by everyone

I've never done Pilates. Is that okay?

That's most people's first class. Take OG Reformer, tell the front desk it's your first time, and your trainer will set up your reformer and cue you through every move.

What's a reformer?

A sliding carriage on springs, with straps and a footbar. The springs add resistance or support, so the same machine makes a move harder or easier. Your trainer sets it for you.

How long is class?

45 minutes. Arrive 10 minutes early for your first one so there's time to get set up.

Do I need grip socks?

Yes, they're required on the reformer for safety. Bring your own or buy a pair at the front desk.

I have an injury or I'm pregnant.

Let your trainer know before class starts. They'll give you options for any move that doesn't work for you, and check with your doctor if you're unsure.

How do I book?

Book on the class schedule or in the Arketa app. Classes are 12 people max, so book ahead for popular times.
