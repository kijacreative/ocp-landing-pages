Email + SMS · New client welcome series

Welcome series

Triggered when someone buys a New Client Special (3 classes for $25, 1-Week Unlimited for $59, or 10 classes for $145). Three emails, each with a text, all pointing first timers to lp.oakcliffpilates.com/welcome. Each email is shown at 600px desktop and 375px mobile. Open one on its own to copy its HTML into Arketa.

Before these send

  • [UNSUBSCRIBE_URL] goes in every footer. Swap it for Arketa's unsubscribe tag. {{clientFirstName}} is Arketa's first-name merge tag.
  • The UPGRADE25 promo code has to exist in Arketa, applied to monthly memberships, before email 2 sends.
  • 1-Week Unlimited buyers already get the 9-email trial sequence. Exclude them from this series, or retire the trial sequence, so nobody gets both.
  • Suppress emails 2 and 3 for anyone who buys a membership. Email 3 should only go to clients without one.
  • The class descriptions and pace levels are drafts written from the class names. Have a trainer check them on the page and in email 1.
  • Texts need SMS consent in Arketa. The day-before text only works if Arketa can trigger off a first booking; skip it if not.
