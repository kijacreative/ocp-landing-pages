Two things about tomorrow

You'll probably be sore somewhere unexpected. That's normal and it's the point. It usually shows up 24 to 36 hours out.

Don't wait for it to pass before going again. A second class inside 48 hours is what turns this from a thing you tried into a thing you do.

Take a different trainer. There are 50+ of them and they teach the same format completely differently. The one you click with is the reason people stay for years, and you won't find them by repeating the same class.
