In 2016 this was free mat classes at Kidd Springs Park.

A speaker in the grass and whoever showed up.

Bishop Arts came in 2021. Uptown in 2024. Lower Greenville last year, built from scratch with 14 reformers, a private training room and a podcast studio in the back.

What didn't change is the room. Lights down, music up, and no mirrors anywhere in any of the three buildings. That last one is deliberate. Mirrors turn a class into a performance, and we'd rather you paid attention to how something feels than how it looks.
