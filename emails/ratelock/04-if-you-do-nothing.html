The fine print, made short

What happens
if you do nothing?

On January 1, an unlimited membership that hasn't chosen moves to All-Studio Unlimited at $229 a month automatically. Nothing breaks, you just pay the new rate. 4, 8 and 12-class memberships stay as they are and go up $10 a month.

If you'd rather keep what you pay now, or switch to a plan that suits you better, choose before December 31.

 
How long is my rate locked?
Choose before December 31, 2026 and your rate holds through December 31, 2027. If rates rise again after that, this rate isn't guaranteed.
Can I pause?
Yes, on every plan, up to twice a year and no more than 30 days in total.
Do my teacher or nurse discounts still apply?
Yes, on monthly plans. They take $10 off per month. They don't apply to prepay.
How does the guest pass work?
Every plan includes a free guest pass each month to bring a friend to class.
Can I switch plans later?
Write to kiel@oakcliffpilates.com and a real person will sort it out.
